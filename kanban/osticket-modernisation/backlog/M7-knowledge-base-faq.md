# M7 — MILESTONE – Knowledge Base & FAQ

- **ID**: M7

## Spec References

- FS-050 (public knowledge base browse/read/search published FAQs; staff FAQ management: create/publish/attach/associate; article + category visibility gating)

## Description

Adds a self-service knowledge base: a public KB where visitors browse, read, and search
published FAQs (gated by article + category visibility), and staff FAQ management to author,
publish, attach files to, and associate FAQs with help topics — deflecting tickets before
they are opened.

## Acceptance Criteria (to be defined as a browser-only E2E during consolidation)
